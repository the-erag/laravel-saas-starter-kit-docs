# ERAG

ERAG (Engineer • Research • Advance • Grow) builds and maintains SaaS Laravel and publishes open-source packages for Laravel, Inertia, Vue and React at [erag.in](https://erag.in). You can read more [about SaaS Laravel and ERAG](/about.html).

Articles signed by ERAG are written by the team that builds the kits. They cover multi-tenancy with a database per customer, roles and permissions, sign-in security, Laravel architecture and code quality, and the day-to-day work of launching and running a SaaS product.
